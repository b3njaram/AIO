# Jobbi Home — proposed limited warranty and return policy / Propuesta de garantía limitada y devoluciones

DRAFT for owner and local legal review before any sale. Not effective until seller's legal identity, service address, phone, and required disclosures are completed and policy delivered before purchase. Mandatory consumer rights supersede these terms. Do not use as an unconditional "no refunds" policy.

## English
**Coverage from accepted delivery:** 12 months for new hardware supplied by JobbiSoft and 90 calendar days for disclosed refurbished hardware supplied by JobbiSoft. Installation/workmanship defects: 90 calendar days, separately from any included remote support period. Any longer promised or legally required coverage applies. Customer-owned hardware is not covered by Jobbi hardware warranty; installation defects remain covered. Manufacturer warranties are additional where valid and transferable; disclose duration and region on each order. Jobbi remains responsible for its own obligations even if its supplier refuses a claim.

**Remedies:** Contact the official support email in CONTACT.md with order ID or other proof of purchase and symptom. Jobbi acknowledges within two business days as a target, diagnoses and provides repair, equivalent replacement or refund in accordance with applicable law and customer rights. Where the law allows the customer's choice of remedy, respect it. No charge for covered work or required warranty transport. If repair/replacement is not feasible within a reasonable or statutory period, provide the applicable refund or other statutory remedy. Document dates and do not delay statutory deadlines while waiting on suppliers. No blanket exclusions of implied warranties.

**Voluntary change-of-mind returns:** Within 14 calendar days from delivery, request return of supplied hardware in reasonably complete condition, with accessories. Refund the hardware price in cash; no restocking fee for standard stocked hardware. Missing/damaged items or special orders require individual assessment and lawful written agreement; never use a fee to defeat cancellation rights. Separately agreed installation labor already performed may be retained only to the extent permitted by law, disclosed and expressly agreed; refund unperformed services. Defective, misdescribed, or legally cancellable orders follow applicable statutory rights regardless of this voluntary window. Refund approved voluntary returns within 7 calendar days of receiving the equipment or sooner if law requires; statutory cancellation refund deadlines take precedence. Refund in original currency; never force store credit.

**Cancellation before delivery:** Full refund of unspent deposit. Any retention for demonstrable nonrecoverable special-order costs requires advance disclosure, express agreement and legal permission; statutory cooling-off/full-refund rights prevail. No blanket nonrefundable deposits. For eligible home/temporary-location sales, deliver the required cancellation notice and form before purchase.

**Limitations:** Accidental damage, misuse, unauthorized modification causing the fault, incompatible customer equipment, normal wear, environmental damage and unrelated third-party outages are generally outside this voluntary warranty, only where legally permissible and causally relevant. No exclusion limits statutory rights. Do not promise lifetime software compatibility, data recovery or zero data loss. The customer should maintain an independent backup; do not require surrender of passwords. Obtain explicit consent before accessing or erasing data, arrange secure drive return/retention where possible, and use a documented transfer receipt. Do not automatically wipe a returned device.

## Español
**Cobertura desde la entrega aceptada:** 12 meses para equipo nuevo suministrado por JobbiSoft y 90 días naturales para equipo reacondicionado suministrado por JobbiSoft y expresamente identificado. Defectos de instalación: 90 días naturales, independientes del periodo de soporte remoto. Prevalece cualquier plazo mayor ofrecido o exigido por ley. No cubre equipo aportado por el cliente, sin afectar la garantía por defectos de instalación. La garantía del fabricante es adicional cuando sea válida y transferible; indicar plazo y región por pedido. Jobbi conserva sus obligaciones aunque el proveedor rechace una reclamación.

**Soluciones:** Contactar al correo de soporte de CONTACT.md con folio u otro comprobante y descripción. Objetivo de respuesta inicial: dos días hábiles. Diagnóstico, reparación, reposición equivalente o devolución conforme a la ley, incluida la elección del consumidor cuando proceda. Sin cargo por trabajo cubierto ni transporte exigido para hacer efectiva la garantía. Si no es viable reparar o sustituir en plazo razonable o legal, aplicar devolución u otro remedio legal. Registrar fechas; no retrasar plazos legales por esperar al proveedor. No se renuncia a derechos obligatorios.

**Devolución voluntaria por cambio de opinión:** Solicitar dentro de 14 días naturales de la entrega; devolver equipo suministrado razonablemente completo, con accesorios. Reembolso en efectivo del precio del equipo; sin cargo de reposición para equipo estándar. Faltantes, daños o pedidos especiales requieren valoración individual y acuerdo permitido por ley; no imponer cargos que anulen el derecho de cancelación. Solo podrá retenerse mano de obra ya realizada cuando sea legal, previamente informada y expresamente aceptada; reembolsar servicios no prestados. Defectos, incumplimientos o cancelaciones legales se atienden conforme a la ley sin importar este plazo voluntario. Reembolso voluntario aprobado dentro de 7 días naturales de recibir el equipo o antes si la ley exige; prevalecen plazos legales. Misma moneda, sin crédito obligatorio en tienda.

**Cancelación antes de entrega:** Devolver anticipo no utilizado. Retenciones por gastos especiales comprobables y no recuperables solo con información previa, aceptación y permiso legal; prevalece revocación legal con devolución total. Sin anticipos absolutamente no reembolsables. Para ventas a domicilio o fuera del establecimiento sujetas a revocación, entregar aviso y formato exigidos antes de contratar.

**Limitaciones:** Daño accidental, mal uso, modificación que cause la falla, equipo incompatible del cliente, desgaste normal, ambiente inadecuado y fallas externas ajenas quedan fuera de esta cobertura voluntaria únicamente cuando sea legal y exista relación causal. No limita derechos obligatorios. No se promete compatibilidad perpetua, recuperación de datos ni pérdida cero. Mantener respaldo independiente. No exigir contraseñas; obtener autorización expresa para acceso o borrado de datos, documentar recepción y procurar devolución segura de discos. No borrar automáticamente equipo devuelto.

## Required pre-sale completion
Seller legal name; service address and phone; order ID, item/serial, new/refurbished condition, coverage duration, geographic scope, claim process, applicable transport and remedies; provide written warranty and any mandatory cancellation notices before sale. Include manufacturer terms where applicable. Review Mexico LFPC articles 51–56, 77–84, 92 and applicable rules; covered remote/home sales may allow revocation within five business days and guarantees generally cannot be shorter than 90 days. In the US, review FTC Magnuson-Moss disclosure rules and Cooling-Off Rule for qualifying in-home/temporary sales, as well as Texas law. Do not present this draft as a substitute for mandatory documents or advice.
