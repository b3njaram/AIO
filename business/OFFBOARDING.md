# Customer independence and offboarding

The customer does not need an ongoing Jobbi payment to keep installed software running. At delivery and again if support ends, make sure they own the device, local admin accounts, tailnet, DNS/domain where applicable, invoices, backups and recovery information. Never retain a shared master credential or remote kill switch.

- Export or copy user-owned files via each app's supported method. Preserve original photo and music libraries, local movie files, Nextcloud user data, task exports and encrypted password-vault exports using upstream instructions. Do not ask the customer to send a plaintext password export.
- Provide the instance's release manifest, deployment snapshot and recovery procedure securely. Keep backup archives encrypted, off-device, and test restore. Confirm licenses and notices for shipped software.
- Remove temporary support accounts and devices, revoke credentials and rotate those previously shared. Confirm customer access before revocation. Do not modify or delete their server if they decline support.
- Return or delete any authorized support copies according to the agreed retention period and applicable law. Keep only required business records. Record the closure privately.
